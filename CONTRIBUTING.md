# Contributing to Aero CMS

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Aero CMS! We're excited to
build a modern, modular CMS for the .NET ecosystem — and we'd love your help.

## Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

**Important:** All contributors must sign a Contributor License Agreement
before their pull requests can be accepted. This is a standard legal
requirement for open-source projects with dual-licensing models.

By signing the CLA, you agree that:

1. You grant us the right to use your contribution under both the
   **Apache License 2.0** and the **GNU AGPL v3** licenses.
2. You retain full ownership of your contribution.
3. Your contribution is your original work.

This ensures we can continue offering Aero CMS under both its open-source
and commercial licenses without legal friction.

### How to Sign

On your first pull request, the CLA Assistant bot will automatically
prompt you to sign. Follow the instructions in the PR comment — it takes
about 30 seconds.

## How to Contribute

1. **Fork the repository** and create your branch from main.
2. **Open an issue** first for significant changes so we can discuss
   the approach before you invest time in code.
3. **Write tests** for any new functionality. We use TUnit for unit
   tests and Playwright for integration tests.
4. **Follow the coding patterns** established in the project:
   - SOLID principles and GoF patterns where appropriate
   - Railway-oriented programming (Result\<T\>, Option\<T\>)
   - Clean architecture with vertical slices
   - No reflection — prefer source generators
5. **Run the tests** before submitting your PR.
6. **Submit a pull request** with a clear description of the change
   and its motivation.

## Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and professional. We're all here to build
something great together.

## Questions?

Open a discussion or issue — we're happy to help.
